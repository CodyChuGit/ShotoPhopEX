# Third-party licences and redistribution requirements

Reviewed 2026-11-07 at the commit of this change (its `Cargo.lock` matches the v0.1.0 tag's,
apart from the two security patch bumps in `SECURITY_AUDIT.md`). Licences come from each
component's own metadata and licence files; a scanner's or a manifest's word is evidence, not
clearance. Status values: **VERIFIED** (declared licence and text found, permissive, compatible
with distribution under MIT OR Apache-2.0), **UNRESOLVED** (see `UNRESOLVED_RISKS.md`),
**INCOMPATIBLE** (none found).

Machine-readable: [`sbom/shotophopex-0.1.0-windows-x64.spdx.json`](sbom/shotophopex-0.1.0-windows-x64.spdx.json)
and [`sbom/shotophopex-0.1.0-linux-x86_64.spdx.json`](sbom/shotophopex-0.1.0-linux-x86_64.spdx.json)
(SPDX 2.3; each crate's declared licence, crates.io download location and purl). They are
regenerated with every release build by `packaging/third_party_notices.py`, which also writes the
full licence texts into `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` in each package.

How this inventory was made: `cargo tree -p photocraft -p photocraft-cli -e normal` for each
release target with the release features (Windows: `heif, alerts, native-diffusion`; Linux:
`native-diffusion`), joined with `cargo metadata`; every crate's package was searched for licence
files; `cargo audit` for advisories; the release packages were unpacked (`RELEASE_COMPLIANCE.md`);
non-crate assets come from `ATTRIBUTION.md`, `NOTICE` and `genai-native/NOTICE`, spot-checked
against the files.

## Summary

- **No copyleft obligation found in shipped code.** The two crates offering a copyleft option
  are used under their permissive alternative: `self_cell` (Apache-2.0 OR GPL-2.0-only →
  Apache-2.0) and `r-efi` (MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR LGPL-2.1-or-later; not in the shipped set). The
  MPL-2.0 crate `colored` is a build/dev dependency only. `webpki-root-certs` data is
  CDLA-Permissive-2.0 (no notice requirement for use).
- **Notice-carrying licences** in the binaries: MIT, BSD-2/3-Clause, ISC, Unicode-3.0, Zlib,
  BSL-1.0, IJG (`jpeg-encoder`), SIL OFL 1.1 and Ubuntu Font Licence (fonts). v0.1.0 shipped
  none of their texts; the next packages do (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`).
- **Apache-2.0 only, not dual**: `genai-native/crates/qir` (umag/qwen-image-rs) and its
  vendored SageAttention/FlashInfer/FasterTransformer kernels. The binaries that link it are
  therefore distributed with Apache-2.0 obligations for that part (licence copy, NOTICE, change
  notices: U-13).
- **Proprietary**: NVIDIA CUDA runtime, cuBLAS, cuBLASLt, cuRAND (redistributable under the CUDA
  EULA, Attachment A; U-16).
- **Not redistributed**: model weights (`MODEL_LICENSES.md`); CUDA toolkit, CCCL, CUTLASS
  sources and the NVIDIA driver (build-time only; CUTLASS templates compiled in carry its
  BSD-3-Clause notice).
